Aesthetic Bliss - Website Plan

Site Name

Aesthetic Bliss: This name reflects a platform focused on enhancing beauty and wellness through medical aesthetic procedures.

Site Purpose

The website will educate visitors about various medical aesthetic treatments and offer products to support post-treatment care and enhance beauty.

Scenarios

  • What aesthetic procedures are best for skin rejuvenation?
  • Where can I buy post-treatment skincare products?

Color Scheme

Primary color: Light pink (#f7d6e0) for background accents and headers.

Secondary color: Dark gray (#333333) for body text and navigation links.

Typography

Font 1: Merriweather for headings.

Font 2: Roboto for body text.
